Instagram

An Instagram post needs a picture, and Instagram has no link that starts a post with words in it. Save the picture below (on a phone, press and hold it), copy the caption, then make the post in Instagram’s app or website and paste the caption in. A web address in a caption can’t be clicked, so put the website’s address in your bio, or on a story’s link sticker, too.

MPE Roll is a free, open-source plugin for FL Studio with its own MPE piano roll. Load your synth inside it (VST3, VST2 or CLAP) and every note gets its own pitch glide, pressure, timbre and controllers, which FL's piano roll can't send to plugins. For FL's own synths it writes a piano roll script that rebuilds the clip with slide notes. Windows and macOS. No ads, no tracking.
